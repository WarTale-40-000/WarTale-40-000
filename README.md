# WarTale 40,000

## The 41st millennium has come to Hytale

Take up arms in the grim darkness of the far future. WarTale brings Warhammer 40,000-inspired weapons, armor, and battlefield gear to Hytale, with custom models, animations, sounds, and gameplay to match.

Gear up as a Cadian or Sirdar, then choose how to fight. Cut through the enemy with a chainsword, hold the line with a lasgun, or bring a flamer when subtlety is no longer an option.

## What's in the mod

- **A growing arsenal:** lasguns, autoguns, bolt pistols, flamers, chainswords, power swords, combat knives, and more.
- **Distinct armor and styles:** Cadian and Sirdar armor, with alternate looks to make your kit your own.
- **Weapons with working reloads:** manage magazines and keep an eye on your ammo HUD.
- **More wargear for your world:** medkits, crafting stations, banners, and statues.
- **Custom-made assets:** models, animations, and weapon sounds built for WarTale.

WarTale is in **alpha**. Content and balance are still changing, and some parts may be unfinished.
